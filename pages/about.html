Our Story

A Restaurant Built Around Family and Freshness

Green House Restaurant was founded with one simple goal: to serve delicious meals prepared with fresh ingredients while creating a warm and welcoming environment for everyone.

Over the years, we've become known for our commitment to quality, exceptional customer service, and authentic Nigerian and continental dishes that keep customers coming back.

Our Mission

To provide freshly prepared meals, outstanding customer service, and memorable dining experiences that exceed expectations every day.

Our Vision

To become one of Nigeria's most trusted restaurant brands by consistently delivering quality meals and exceptional hospitality.
